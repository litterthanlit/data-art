What you are looking at

Every point is a real galaxy or quasar measured by the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument. Light from far away is stretched toward red on its journey. Astronomers call that stretch redshift, and it tells us how long the light has been travelling.

Each object sits at the depth of its own travel time and wears its own colour: deep red light left billions of years ago, white and blue-white light is younger. The faint streaks are light leaving each object for the core, which stands for here and now. They fade before they arrive.
